Kaya kahit sinabi mo na kung ano talaga yung standard mo, I tried to change myself. Kumain ako nang kumain, nag-try ako mag-lift, and I tried my best na maabot yung standards na akala ko kailangan kong abutin para magustuhan mo ako. Pero habang tumatagal, napaisip ako... why would I keep changing myself for someone na baka hindi naman ako mahalin kung sino talaga ako?

Kaya kahit mahaba yung progress, tinuloy ko lang. I tried my best to make you fall for me. And honestly, akala ko wala na talagang pag-asa. Akala ko trip-trip mo lang ako. Akala ko hanggang sa gano'n lang tayo.

Pero then came the day na finally nag-meet tayo.
And that was the moment na narealize ko kung ano talaga yung pinasok ko.

Every minute na magkasama tayo sa Pampanga, I genuinely cherished. And if going back and forth from here just to be with you means spending hours traveling just to have a little more time with you, kayang-kaya kong bumiyahe. Kahit pagod, kahit hassle, gagawin ko. Just to spend time with you.

And I don't even know why the fuck I'm crying while writing this right now.

And I guess may reason din kung bakit nandiyan ka ngayon sa labas ng convento. And maybe that reason was me. I know mas mahal mo si Lord, and I respect that so much. Pero gusto ko lang sabihin na mahal na mahal din kita.

Even though LDR tayo ngayon, and alam nating marami pa tayong pagdadaanan at maraming years pa bago natin makasama ang isa't isa nang mas madalas, I'm willing to wait. I'm willing to take the distance, the waiting, the calls, the missed moments, and everything in between—until the day na finally, we can be together more peacefully.

I never realized na love could actually feel this peaceful.

May chaos, may tampuhan, may insecurities, may moments na nami-miss natin isa't isa sobra. Pero ever since I met you, there's this kind of peace na hindi ko ma-explain. Yung feeling na kahit ang daming nangyayari, may isang taong gusto kong uwian, kausapin, at mahalin.

And honestly, hindi ko na papahabain pa kasi alam mo naman kung gaano kita kamahal.

Mahal na mahal na mahal kita, bub.
